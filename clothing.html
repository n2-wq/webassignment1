Baby Clothing Care Tips

🧺

Wash Before First Use

Always wash new clothes before your baby wears them to remove any manufacturing residues.

🌡️

Use Cold Water

Cold water is gentler on baby fabrics and helps prevent shrinkage and color fading.

🌿

Mild Detergent Only

Use fragrance-free, baby-safe detergents to protect your baby's sensitive skin.
