About BabyFactory

Your trusted baby store in Deer Park Mall

Our Story

BabyFactory is a small, upscale shop located in Deer Park Mall, specializing in high-quality clothing, toys, and accessories for infants and toddlers. We believe that every baby deserves the best, and we carefully select each product for its safety, quality, and adorable design.

Our Mission

To provide parents and gift-givers with a curated selection of premium baby products that are both beautiful and functional, delivered with warm, knowledgeable customer service.

❤️

Quality First

We select only the safest, highest-quality products for your baby.

🌱

Safe & Natural

Our Journey

2022

The Idea

Founder Sarah, a new mom, struggled to find quality baby products in one place. The idea for BabyFactory was born.

2023

Opening Day

BabyFactory opened its doors at Deer Park Mall with just 50 products and a big dream.

2024

Growing Fast

We expanded our range to over 200 products and launched our loyalty program for returning customers.

2026

Online Launch

We launched our website to serve parents across New Zealand, not just in Palmerston North.
